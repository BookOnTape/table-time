# Design review, 12 Sep 2026

Four skill-driven review passes over the app as of commit `32ac341`. Findings are
grouped by what to do with them, not by which review found them. Line numbers
are approximate; grep the quoted identifier.

Legend: **fix** = clear defect, do it. **decide** = judgment call, owner picks.

## Fix: safety and error recovery

- **Settings stay unlocked after Back.** `src/lib/settings.tsx` keeps `unlocked`
  in memory and `SettingsPage` only clears it on the explicit Lock button. A
  parent who leaves with Back hands the child an open settings page. Lock on
  unmount (or on any navigation away from `/settings`).
- **Doodle Pad Clear is one tap, no undo, and Back destroys the drawing.**
  Add an undo stack (last N strokes) or a confirm on Clear; persist the canvas
  to storage like the coloring pages.
- **Coloring "start over" uses the undo glyph and wipes the saved page.** Either
  make it a real single-step undo, or use a distinct glyph and confirm.
- **Passcode confirm mismatch gives no message**, only a shake, and leaves the
  user on the confirm step. Show "Didn't match. Try again." and return to the
  new-passcode step.
- **Tic-tac-toe's always-visible "New game" under the board** is a mis-tap that
  wipes a game. Move it into the toolbar with the same undo/restart affordance
  as the other activities.

## Fix: touch targets (44px minimum, 56 preferred)

- Crayons narrow to ~31px with nine in a row on a 390px phone
  (`Crayon.css` `min-width: 30px`). Options: two rows, a horizontal scroll, or
  drop to eight crayons in the doodle pad.
- Brush-size buttons 44x36 (`doodle-pad/style.css`).
- Settings switches 52x32 and the row `div` is not tappable; make each row a
  `label` so the whole 56px row toggles.
- "See all" links 40px (`Home.css`), moot if section pages are cut (see decide).
- Coloring star and bubble regions (r=13 to 16 on a 400 viewBox) render at
  ~25px. Enlarge to r>=24 or give small regions an invisible larger hit path.

## Fix: contrast and text

- `--ink-3` is 2.4:1 on paper and is used for text (eyebrows, readout labels,
  shelf counts, `.cbn__of`, the bubble hint, placeholders). Make `--ink-3`
  decorative-only and use `--ink-2` (4.9:1) for any text.
- `outline: none` on the settings name input kills the focus ring.
- Headings inherit body line-height 1.4; set 1.1 to 1.2 for display sizes.

## Fix: glass and motion

- **Glass on glass on glass in `Celebrate`:** overlay blur, card blur, and
  `.btn--quiet` blur. Card buttons should be fills (ink or paper), not glass.
- **Reduced motion breaks Bubble Pop:** the blanket 0.01ms rule makes bubbles
  arrive instantly and "escape" before a tap; `cbnMarch` becomes an infinite
  0.01ms loop. Exempt functional motion from the blanket rule (slow bubbles,
  static hint outline) and never leave an `infinite` animation at 0.01ms.
- No `prefers-contrast: more` handling; `.glass` should become an opaque
  surface with a border there.
- Optical size is misapplied: DM Sans loads `wght` only, and setting
  `font-variation-settings: "opsz"` on `body` disables automatic optical sizing
  for Bricolage everywhere it isn't overridden. Remove the opsz settings and rely
  on `font-optical-sizing: auto`.
- Landscape safe areas ignored: add `env(safe-area-inset-left/right)` to
  `--gutter`.

## Fix: copy and naming (one word per thing)

- The parent screen is called "Parent settings", "Open settings", "Parents",
  and "Settings". Use **Parents** everywhere.
- Restart is "Start over", "Color again", "Play again", "New game", "Clear".
  In play: the same restart glyph and aria "Start over" in every activity.
  After a win: "Play again". Doodle: "Clear" stays (it is a different verb).
- "float off" vs "float away"; pick "float away". Cut the in-game bubble hint;
  a toddler can't read it and bubbles arrive in under a second.
- Registry blurbs: "Color by number scenes" -> "Pictures to color by number";
  "Finger paint with crayons" -> "Draw with crayons"; the Color by Number blurb
  is dead (flattened activities never render it), delete it.
- Reset progress: replace native `confirm()` (prints the origin on iOS
  standalone) with the app's own sheet: "Clear coloring pages and best scores?"
  with **Clear** / **Cancel**; row label "Clear progress"; toast "Progress
  cleared".
- "Everything is stored on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere." ->
  "Everything stays on this phone."
- Segmented labels on child screens are text-only (Slow/Fast, Easy/Medium/Hard,
  By number/Any color). Add glyphs; only tic-tac-toe pairs icons with text.

## Fix: small correctness and a11y

- Back button pushes (`navigate(backTo)`) instead of popping; with history
  available, use `navigate(-1)` so hardware back works.
- `role="gridcell"` without a `row` parent in tic-tac-toe; `aria-label` on a
  plain `div` in PinPad (wants `role="status"`); `Celebrate` dialog lacks
  `aria-modal` and focus move.
- Toolbar can overflow at larger system text: `.seg__opt` nowrap with no
  `min-width: 0`, `.readout` nowrap. Allow the toolbar to wrap.
- `theme-color` in `index.html` is iOS grey, not `--paper`.
- Remove dead exports: `countForSection`, the unused variant-picker branch in
  `PlayPage` (only if the section-page decision below keeps it dead).

## Decide: structure

- **Cut section pages and "See all" while there are nine tiles.** Home becomes
  one grid with three light headings. "Create" currently links to a grid of
  one. Bring sections back past ~12 tiles. Also fixes "Back goes somewhere the
  kid never was" (Home -> Rocket -> Back lands on `/section/coloring`).
- Shelf count badges inform nobody; cut.
- Celebration card hides the finished picture, which is why "Keep looking"
  exists. A non-blocking banner removes both.
- Bubble Pop speed toggle: ramp speed with score instead.
- Memory Match has two controls for difficulty (segmented + "Harder"); keep one.
- "Name" setting only feeds a greeting the child can't read. Keep if the parent
  values it.
- "Ages 3+" meta in settings is not actionable.
- Default passcode printed on the lock screen; fine for a toddler, not an older
  sibling. Nudge once toward Change passcode, or stop printing it after the
  first unlock.
- `user-scalable=no`: defensible for the play surface, not for Parents.
- Tic-tac-toe's win overlay blurs the board so the winning line is never seen;
  delay the card ~600ms or show it as a banner.
- Spacing and type scales are ad hoc (15 text sizes incl. 13.5px and 12.5px; no
  spacing tokens; `--gutter: 20px` is off any scale). Worth one tokenization
  pass when touching CSS anyway; not urgent on its own.
- Shadows are one size for everything (46px buttons and full-width groups
  share `--glass-shadow`). Scale by elevation when doing the tokenization pass.

## Fix: feedback (game-feel audit)

No haptics exist (web). Channels are motion and audio only.

- **Memory Match's match animation hides the matched card.** `mm__card--matched
  .mm__face` runs `pop`, whose keyframes replace the face's resting
  `rotateY(180deg)` with `scale()` and start at opacity 0; with
  `backface-visibility: hidden` the card vanishes ~320ms then snaps back. Animate
  a wrapper or include the rotate in the keyframes.
- **The celebration overlay mounts in the same frame as the winning move**, so
  nobody sees the three-in-a-row tint, the last flip, or the last fill. Delay
  the overlay ~700ms after the win state.
- **Double-fire on the climax.** Memory Match fires `pop` and `win` for one
  event from two places; tic-tac-toe and coloring fire `tap` plus `win`, and the
  660Hz tap masks the 523Hz first note of the fanfare. Suppress the action sound
  on the game-ending move; one owner per event.
- **Loudness gradient is inverted** in `src/lib/sound.ts`: pop 0.12 > win 0.10 >
  tap 0.08 > nope 0.05. The most frequent sound (bubble pop, up to twice a
  second) is the loudest; the fanfare is quieter. In a restaurant this is the
  wrong way round. Order: tap < nope < pop < win, and cap overlapping pops.
- **Coloring win fires on derived state, not on an event.** Reopening a finished
  scene mounts straight into the overlay and calls `win()` outside a user
  gesture (iOS keeps the AudioContext suspended, so it is silent anyway); toggling
  the mode on a full page re-triggers it. Fire on the completing tap only.
- Reduced-motion breaks Bubble Pop (also listed under glass and motion).

Decide:
- Readout numbers teleport; a short count-up would help but is low impact.
- Memory mismatch and Bubble Pop escapes are silent; taps during the 750ms lock,
  on filled cells, or during the robot's turn acknowledge nothing. Quiet misses
  suit toddlers; zero acknowledgment of a tap does not.
- Tie uses the same card and sound as a win; a tie should be smaller.
- Doodle Clear plays the match sound. Give destructive actions their own cue.
- After a number completes, the crayon stays selected and the next tap earns a
  `nope` for the app's own state; auto-advance to the next unfinished crayon.
- Sound toggle is behind the parent passcode with no in-activity mute; the iOS
  ringer switch is the de facto policy. Document that or add a mute button.

## What is right, leave it

Two taps from Home to play. One chrome for every activity. Big color tiles are
the only tappable things on the child surface. Press feedback everywhere.
Self-hosted fonts with real fallbacks. Sheets and boards are opaque; tint is
limited to one accent button per celebration; segmented thumbs are fills, not
nested glass; board cells are concentric. Celebration copy ("Beautiful.",
"All matched.", "Red wins.") has a consistent voice.
